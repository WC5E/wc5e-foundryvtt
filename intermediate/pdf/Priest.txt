                                                                       The Light and the Void
                                                                       The two sources of power that a priest can draw
                                                                       upon--the Light and the Void--are diametrically
                                                                       opposed. In fact, it is believed by some that it was
                                                                       the mixing of the two energy sources which led to a
                                                                       series of cosmic-level explosions that gave birth to
                                                                       the physical universe. A devout Holy priest would
                                                                       not dare draw upon the power of the Void, nor could
                                                                       a powerful acolyte of Shadow draw deep upon the
                                                                       Light without being burned.
                                                                          As such, while mechanically there are few restrict­-
                                                                       ions in the priest's spells and abilities enforcing this
                                                                       divide, players are encouraged to pick either the
                                                                       Light or the Void as their source of power and stick
                                                                       to abilities that match as much as possible--with
                                                                       certain notable exceptions such as discipline priests.
                                                                          Light-based spells are those that produce bright
                                                                       light, deal radiant damage, restore hit points or life
                                                                       to the dead, wound or destroy the undead, or inspire
                                                                       hope and positive emotions. Void-based spells
                                                                       create dark­ness, deal necrotic or psychic damage,
                                                                       drain life, cause sickness, dominate the mind, or
                                                                       cause fear or other negative emotions.
