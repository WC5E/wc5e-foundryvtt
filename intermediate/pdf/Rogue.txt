                                                                   A Simple Code
                                                                   For rogues, the only code is the contract, and their honor is
                                                                   purchased in gold. Free from the constraints of a
                                                                   conscience, these mercenaries rely on brutal and efficient
                                                                   tactics. Lethal assassins and masters of stealth, they will
                                                                   approach their marks from behind, piercing a vital organ
                                                                   and vanishing into the shadows before the victim hits the
                                                                   ground. Rogues can dip their weapons in paralyzing toxins
                                                                   that render foes unable to defend themselves. These silent
                                                                   stalkers wear leather armor so they can move
                                                                   unencumbered, ensuring that they land the first strike.
                                                                     With the rogue’s poisons and speed, the first strike is
                                                                   often the last step before the killing blow.
                                                                     Rogues fend for themselves, looking for fights in which
                                                                   they dictate the terms. They’re the shadows in the night
                                                                   that remain unseen until the right moment comes to strike,
                                                                   then they dispatch an opponent with quick blade work or a
                                                                   deadly toxin snuck acutely into the bloodstream. Rogues
                                                                   are opportunistic thieves, bandits, and assassins, but
                                                                   there’s an unparalleled art to what they do.

Subtlety
Subtlety rogues are the masters of the shadows, and they
strike unseen. Some claim the art of subtlety looks like
malevolent shadow magic — these rogues are capable of
performing devastating assaults on their enemies, slipping
away unharmed to strike again without detection. Most
rogues train their entire lives to learn how to walk in the
shadows — subtlety rogues were born there.
Subtle Magic
When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you learn to
harness magic from the shadows. See chapter 10 of the
Player's Handbook for the general rules of spellcasting.
   Spell Slots. You have a number of spell slots equal to
your Intelligence modifier + half your rogue level. You can
expend these spell slots only to cast spells through Subtle
Magic. Spells cast this way are always cast at their lowest
spell level, and doesn't require any components. You regain
all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.
   Spells Known of 1st-Level and Higher. At 3rd level, you
know how to subtly cast the fog cloud and feather fall spell.
As you gain levels in this class you learn know to cast addi-
tional spells through this feature, as shown in the Subtle
Magic Spells table.

Subterfuge
At 3rd level, your thoughts can't be read by telepathy or
other means, unless you allow it. You can present false
thoughts by making a Charisma (Deception) check
contested by the mind reader's Wisdom (Insight) check.
Shadowstep
Upon reaching 9th level, you gain the ability to step from
one shadow into another. When you are in dim light or
darkness, as a bonus action you can teleport up to 60 feet
to an empty space you can see that is also in dim light or
darkness. You then have advantage on the first melee
attack you make before the end of your turn.
Cloak of Shadows
Beginning at 13th level, you can use your reaction when
you take damage from an attack or spell to step into the
shadows, giving you immunity to all damage until the end
of the next turn, including the triggering attack.
   You can use this feature twice. You regain expended uses
when you finish a short or long rest.
Shadowy Duel
At 17th level, when you hit a creature with a sneak attack,
you can spend 2 energy points to attempt to shroud their
vision in a dense dark mist. The target must succeed on a
Wisdom saving throw or have everything, except you,
become heavily obscured for 1 minute.
   At the end of each of its turns, the target can make
another Wisdom saving throw, ending the shadowy duel on
a successful saving throw.
